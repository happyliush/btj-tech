Start with a sample, then a run from 10 pieces

18 September 2026

Most projects at Shenzhen Bateja Technology Co., Ltd. begin with a model that already exists. You tell us the model and what the brand needs to change. We confirm the sample with you before a production run.

Small batches start at 10 pieces. Ten pieces on a current model is a real order. It is not a lead-in to a larger commitment. A new mold is quoted first, and you pay that tooling cost before we open it. Sample cost, freight, and lead time are confirmed with the sales team. They are not published as a fixed price, because the scope changes with color, packaging, and tooling.

A useful first message names the model, the destination market, and whether you want a current model as-is or a custom version. Europe, the United States, Japan, and Korea are the markets we build for.
