Instant print or digital: which line to brief

6 August 2026

Instant-print cameras make a paper photo in the camera. On this line that is usually thermal paper, so there is no ink cartridge to replace. Digital cameras keep photos and video on the device for playback on the screen.

Choose instant print when the gift is the paper photo: parties, travel sets, and character cameras that print on the spot. Choose digital when the listing is about the screen, a second lens, or a shape such as a thumb camera or a phone-shaped camera.

The two lines can share a brand color and a package. They should not share one spec sheet. Print models need paper size and print resolution. Digital models need the screen size and whether there is a second camera.
