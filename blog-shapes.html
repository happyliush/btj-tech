Thumb, phone-shaped, and character cameras

4 February 2026

The digital line is not one shape. Thumb cameras are the small body a child wears or holds in one hand. Phone-shaped cameras use a larger screen and often a touch surface. Character cameras use a unicorn, dinosaur, bear, or similar housing on either a print model or a digital model.

If the listing photo is the reason people buy, keep that housing and change the logo and package. If the brand needs its own character, that is a new shell, and it should be sampled before a batch.
