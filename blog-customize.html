What can change on a current model

12 June 2026

If you cover the cost, the factory can change the current design. Typical changes are the logo, housing color, packaging, and the language of the manual.

On the camera itself, the startup screen and the on-screen UI can be changed. A new shape, a new button layout, or a new mold is also possible. That is a tooling project, so the sample comes before the 10-piece run.

Send a reference photo or the model name, then mark what must stay the same. Keeping the current housing is the shorter path. Opening a new mold is the path when the brand needs its own silhouette.
