Instant print or digital: which line to brief

6 August 2026

Instant-print cameras make a paper photo in the camera. On this line that is usually thermal paper, so there is no ink cartridge to replace. Digital cameras keep photos and video on the device for playback on the screen.

Choose instant print when the gift is the paper photo: parties, travel sets, and character cameras that print on the spot. Choose digital when the listing is about the screen, a second lens, or a shape such as a thumb camera or a phone-shaped camera.

The two lines can share a brand color and a package. They should not share one spec sheet. Print models need paper size and print resolution. Digital models need the screen size and whether there is a second camera.

What can change on a current model

12 June 2026

If you cover the cost, the factory can change the current design. Typical changes are the logo, housing color, packaging, and the language of the manual.

On the camera itself, the startup screen and the on-screen UI can be changed. A new shape, a new button layout, or a new mold is also possible. That is a tooling project, so the sample comes before the 10-piece run.

Send a reference photo or the model name, then mark what must stay the same. Keeping the current housing is the shorter path. Opening a new mold is the path when the brand needs its own silhouette.

How to read a kids camera spec

20 March 2026

Start with the screen. A 2.0 inch and a 2.4 inch IPS screen are different products, even when the housing looks similar. If the spec says dual camera, there is a second lens, usually for a selfie. That is not the same as a dual screen.

On instant-print models, look for the paper and the print head. Many current models use 57×30 mm thermal paper and a 200 DPI head. The battery capacity changes how many prints you get before a charge. Standby time on the spec sheet is the number to compare, not a marketing sentence.

Video resolution and photo megapixels only belong in the title when they are written in that model’s spec. A VGA or 1 MP kids camera should not be described as 48 MP. Use the specification table on the product page as the source.

Thumb, phone-shaped, and character cameras

4 February 2026

The digital line is not one shape. Thumb cameras are the small body a child wears or holds in one hand. Phone-shaped cameras use a larger screen and often a touch surface. Character cameras use a unicorn, dinosaur, bear, or similar housing on either a print model or a digital model.

If the listing photo is the reason people buy, keep that housing and change the logo and package. If the brand needs its own character, that is a new shell, and it should be sampled before a batch.

Knowledge base

Answers we use when a buyer writes in.

What is the minimum order?

Small batches start at 10 pieces. A sample comes first when the camera, the package, or the tooling is custom. Sample cost and lead time are confirmed with the sales team.

Which markets do you build for?

Europe, the United States, Japan, and Korea. Tell us the destination country in the first message so packaging language and shipping can be confirmed.

Why do many kids print cameras use thermal paper?

Thermal paper needs no ink. That keeps the refill as a roll of paper and avoids an ink cartridge in a children’s product. On our instant-print models, check the paper size on the spec table. Many current models use 57×30 mm paper.

Can the logo and the screen be ours?

Yes, when you cover the cost. Logo, color, packaging, manual language, startup screen, and UI can be changed. A new mold is a separate tooling step and is sampled before production.
