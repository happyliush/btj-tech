Knowledge base

Answers we use when a buyer writes in.

What is the minimum order?

Small batches start at 10 pieces. A sample comes first when the camera, the package, or the tooling is custom. Sample cost and lead time are confirmed with the sales team.

Which markets do you build for?

Europe, the United States, Japan, and Korea. Tell us the destination country in the first message so packaging language and shipping can be confirmed.

Why do many kids print cameras use thermal paper?

Thermal paper needs no ink. That keeps the refill as a roll of paper and avoids an ink cartridge in a children’s product. On our instant-print models, check the paper size on the spec table. Many current models use 57×30 mm paper.

Can the logo and the screen be ours?

Yes, when you cover the cost. Logo, color, packaging, manual language, startup screen, and UI can be changed. A new mold is a separate tooling step and is sampled before production.
